Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

This policy explains what information the Roatán Water Taxi app and website ("we", "us") collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It covers the guest app, the captain app and roatanwatertaxi.com.

Short version: we collect what we need to get you on a boat and keep you safe. We never see or store your card number. Your phone's location stays on your phone unless you are a captain on shift. You can delete your account in the app at any time.

1. Information we collect

WhatWhenWhy
Account: a random account ID, and your email if you choose to sign in with oneWhen you open the appTo keep your trips, tickets and passes together
Profile (optional): name, phone, WhatsApp, emergency contact, languageIf you add them under AccountSo your captain or our team can reach you, and for safety
Trips: pickup and drop-off docks, times, number of riders, kids or towels requested, tours booked, ship name if you are a cruise guestWhen you bookTo run the ride or tour and get you back to your ship on time
Payments: amount, status, a reference from the payment company, and for Zelle the memo code and bank confirmation numberWhen you payTo confirm and refund payments. Card details are entered on the payment company's page; we never receive them
Location (guests)Only if you allow it, while the app is openTo find the dock nearest you. It is used on your phone and is not sent to us
Location (captains)While a captain is on shift, including in the backgroundTo show the boat on the map and dispatch rides. It stops when the captain goes off shift
Community posts: your display name, text, photos, videos, links, dive spots and species you tagIf you postTo show them to other guests. Posts are visible to other app users
Fish scanner photosWhen you scanSent to our AI provider to name the animal. We do not keep the photo unless you post it
Messages: questions to Isla (our assistant), support requests, feedback and reviewsWhen you send themTo answer you and improve the app
Reports and blocksIf you report a post or block someoneTo keep the community safe
Partner applications: name, WhatsApp, business detailsIf you apply as a captain or businessTo review your application

We do not use advertising trackers, we do not sell your information, and we do not build advertising profiles.

2. How we use it

  • To book, run and support your rides, tours and passes.
  • To take payments, send receipts, give refunds and ride credits.
  • To send trip updates and occasional offers to your in-app Inbox. Offer notifications are off unless you turn them on in Settings.
  • To keep guests and captains safe: verified captains, safety reports, emergency contacts.
  • To moderate the community and act on reports within 24 hours.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, and to meet legal and tax obligations.

3. Who we share it with

Only with service providers that help us run the app, under contract, and only what they need:

  • Supabase: hosts our database, sign-in and file storage (United States).
  • Our payment processor: takes card, Apple Pay and Google Pay payments on its own secure page. If you pay by Zelle, the transfer is between your bank and ours.
  • Anthropic: powers Isla and the fish scanner. It receives your question or photo, not your name or account details.
  • Vercel: hosts roatanwatertaxi.com.
  • Expo: delivers the app and its updates.
  • Apple and Google: provide maps and app store services.
  • Your captain or tour operator: sees your first name, number of riders, dock and time; your WhatsApp only after you pay, so they can find you.

Exchange rates come from a public rates service; no personal information is sent to it. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

4. How long we keep it

  • Account and profile: until you delete your account.
  • Trips and payments: as long as tax and accounting law requires, even after you delete your account (without your name or contact details).
  • Community posts: until you delete them or your account. Posts hidden after reports are kept for review.
  • Captain location: only the latest position is kept.

5. Your choices and rights

  • See and change your profile under Account → Profile.
  • Delete your account under Account → Profile → Delete my account. This removes your profile, saved places, Inbox, community posts and media.
  • Location: turn it off at any time in your phone's settings; you can still pick a dock by hand.
  • Offer notifications: on or off in Account → Settings.
  • Block or report anyone in the community with the ⋯ menu on a post.
  • To ask for a copy of your information, or anything else, email roatanexperienceapp@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

6. Security

Every table in our database is locked to its owner by default. Prices and payment status are set only by our server, never by the app. Card payments happen on the processor's page. No system is perfect; if we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and the community is for users 13 and older. Bookings must be made by an adult. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

8. International transfers

We operate in Honduras and our service providers store data in the United States. By using the app you understand your information will be processed there.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
