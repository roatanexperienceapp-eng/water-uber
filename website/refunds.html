Cancellations & Refunds

Effective October 29, 2026

SituationWhat happens
You cancel a water or land taxi before a captain or driver acceptsFull refund, automatically.
You cancel after a captain or driver acceptsNo refund; they are already on the way.
We are more than 10 minutes late for a scheduled pickupA $10 ride credit is added to your account automatically.
We cancel for weather or safetyYou choose a full refund or a new time.
A group booking is not fully paid 60 minutes before the startIt is cancelled and everyone who paid is refunded automatically.
Our own tours: you cancel 24 hours or more before the startFull refund.
Our own tours: you cancel less than 24 hours before, or do not show upNo refund, unless we can fill your place.
The port van for cruise guestsRefunded together with the tour.
Week passes that have not started yetFull refund. Once started, passes are not refundable.
Tours and rentals by other businessesThat business's cancellation terms apply. Contact us through Help and we will sort it out with them.

How refunds are paid

  • Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay refunds go back to the same card. Banks usually show them within 5–10 days.
  • Zelle payments are refunded by Zelle to the account that paid.
  • Ride credits are used automatically on your next ride.

How to ask

Open the trip in Trips, or go to Account → Help in the app, or email roatanexperienceapp@gmail.com. We usually answer within a few hours.
