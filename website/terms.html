5. Cancellations, delays and refunds

See our Refund Policy. In short: cancel a water taxi before a captain accepts and you are refunded automatically; if we are more than 10 minutes late for a scheduled pickup you get a ride credit; if we cancel for weather or safety, you choose a full refund or a new time.

6. Safety on the water

  • Follow your captain's instructions at all times. Life jackets are on every boat; children must wear one.
  • The captain may refuse or end a trip if conditions are unsafe, a boat is overloaded, or a passenger is intoxicated, aggressive or breaks the law. If you are refused for your own conduct, no refund is due.
  • Sea, weather and health risks come with boating, snorkeling and fishing. Tell us before booking about medical conditions that matter. Do not touch or feed marine life; the Bay Islands National Marine Park has rules that protect it.
  • In an emergency, call 911 first.

If we cancel the tour, the van fare is refunded with it.

The app only offers times that should get you back to the port before all-aboard, using your ship's published schedule. Ship times can change; you remain responsible for being back on board on time. We are not responsible for a missed ship except where it is caused by our own failure to provide a booked ride.
